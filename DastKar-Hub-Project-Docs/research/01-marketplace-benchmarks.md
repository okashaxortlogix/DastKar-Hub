# Marketplace Benchmark Notes

## Daraz

Daraz demonstrates several marketplace patterns worth studying:
- seller onboarding
- seller center
- product listing
- seller performance
- payments/payouts
- marketing tools
- nationwide fulfillment
- seller training/support

DastKar should borrow the *product patterns*, not Daraz's branding or exact UI.

## Alibaba

Alibaba demonstrates:
- customizable seller storefronts
- catalog tooling
- product discovery/search
- seller analytics
- order management
- buyer/seller communication
- logistics and payment tooling
- business protection concepts

DastKar can adapt these patterns to a Pakistani handmade-maker marketplace.

## DastKar differentiation

Instead of maximizing generic product assortment, DastKar should optimize for:
- genuine makers
- maker stories
- handmade/custom products
- trust
- customization
- social seller acquisition
- simple maker operations
