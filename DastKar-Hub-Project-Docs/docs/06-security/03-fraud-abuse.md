# Fraud & Abuse Controls

## Seller abuse

Monitor:
- fake listings
- stolen photos
- fake reviews
- fake orders
- repeated cancellations
- unusual payout behavior
- multiple accounts
- prohibited products

## Buyer abuse

Monitor:
- repeated COD failures
- refund abuse
- repeated claims
- promotion abuse
- account farming
- suspicious payment behavior

## Controls

Use:
- risk flags
- transaction limits
- manual review
- progressive restrictions
- payout holds where contractually/legal appropriate
- audit logs

Do not create opaque permanent bans without an internal review process.
