# Moderation Policy Blueprint

## Remove/restrict

- illegal products
- prohibited products
- counterfeit goods
- stolen content
- fraudulent listings
- materially misleading descriptions
- dangerous products
- manipulated reviews
- abusive content

## Seller appeal

Provide:
- reason
- evidence
- appeal mechanism
- status
- final decision record

## Product moderation

Automated checks may flag.
Human review handles uncertain/high-risk cases.
