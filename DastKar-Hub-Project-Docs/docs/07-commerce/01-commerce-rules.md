# Commerce Rules

## Seller eligibility

Seller must:
- meet identity requirements
- pass maker verification
- accept seller agreement
- provide payout information
- comply with prohibited-product rules

## Product eligibility

Product must:
- be legal to sell
- be accurately described
- use appropriate images
- disclose material/product information
- meet category-specific requirements

## Customized products

Customer selects structured options.
Seller receives exact customization.
Seller confirms feasibility and lead time.

## Standard vs customized

Standard:
- normal return policy subject to category rules.

Customized:
- generally restricted change-of-mind returns
- defect/wrong-item/non-conforming cases remain covered by applicable policy/law.

## Off-platform transactions

DastKar should provide enough value to keep transactions on-platform:
- buyer protection
- reviews
- tracking
- secure payment
- dispute resolution
- seller reputation
