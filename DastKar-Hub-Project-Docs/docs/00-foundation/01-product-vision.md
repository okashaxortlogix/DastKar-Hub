# Product Vision

## Vision

DastKar Hub becomes the trusted digital home for Pakistan's independent makers.

## Mission

Help genuine makers turn their craft into sustainable online businesses while helping customers discover, trust, customize and purchase products from real makers.

## Core promise

### For makers
"Sell your craft without needing to become a full-time e-commerce expert."

### For buyers
"Discover genuine makers and buy with confidence."

## Product pillars

1. Maker identity
2. Trust
3. Discovery
4. Commerce
5. Customization
6. Seller tools
7. Reliable fulfillment
8. Data-driven growth
9. Carefully applied AI

## What DastKar is not

- Not a generic marketplace for everything.
- Not primarily a social network.
- Not primarily an AI product.
- Not a dropshipping platform.
- Not a directory with no transaction layer.

## North-star outcome

A buyer discovers a maker, completes a trusted transaction, receives the product successfully, leaves a verified review, and later returns to buy again.

A maker receives incremental sales and chooses to continue using DastKar because the platform reduces the work required to run online commerce.
