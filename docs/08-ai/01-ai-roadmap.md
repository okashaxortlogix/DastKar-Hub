# AI Roadmap

## Principle

AI is an accelerator, not the product identity.

## Phase 1 AI — Listing Assistant

Input:
- product photos
- seller-provided facts
- simple notes

Output:
- title
- description
- tags
- category suggestion
- social caption

Human approval required.

## Phase 2 AI — Process-to-Reel

Input:
- 3–5 process images/video clips
- seller-approved facts

Output:
- short promotional video
- captions
- optional narration

Must not fabricate product facts.

## Phase 3 AI — Visual Search

Buyer uploads an image.
System returns:
- visually similar products
- similar makers
- customizable alternatives

Never promise exact match unless verified.

## Phase 4 AI

- seller assistant
- demand insights
- personalized discovery
- catalog quality assistant
- customer support assistant

## AI safety rules

Never invent:
- materials
- certifications
- origin
- health claims
- safety claims
- dimensions
- quality claims
- authenticity

All generated marketplace content should have an approval path.
