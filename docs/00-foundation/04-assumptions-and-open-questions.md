# Assumptions & Open Questions

## Business assumptions to validate

- Makers will share DastKar storefront links with their existing audiences.
- Buyers value verified makers and trustworthy transactions.
- A focused handmade category can achieve enough supply density for launch.
- Buyers will pay for unique/custom products online.
- Sellers will accept marketplace commission in exchange for incremental sales and infrastructure.
- A web-first MVP can prove the model before native apps.

## Numbers that must come from experiments

- Conversion rate
- Seller activation rate
- Seller retention
- Customer CAC
- Seller acquisition cost
- AOV
- Repeat purchase rate
- Return/refund rate
- COD failure rate
- Contribution margin per order
- AI cost per seller/order
- Support cost per order
