# Product Principles

1. **Commerce before complexity**
   Every feature should improve discovery, trust, conversion, fulfillment, retention, or seller productivity.

2. **Maker-first**
   Seller workflows must be understandable to a person who is good at their craft but not necessarily good at technology.

3. **Trust is a feature**
   Verification, reviews, policies, accurate listings and transparent order states are product features.

4. **Progressive complexity**
   Keep the first screen simple. Reveal advanced controls when needed.

5. **Fast by default**
   Optimize images, API payloads, database queries and bundle size.

6. **Human-looking, not AI-looking**
   Avoid excessive gradients, floating blobs, glowing borders, futuristic illustrations and generic AI aesthetics.

7. **Mobile-first**
   Many sellers will manage their business from phones.

8. **Explicit state**
   Every order, payment, shipment, refund and verification process needs a clear state.

9. **AI assists; humans approve**
   Generative features must not silently publish factual claims.

10. **Measure before scaling**
    Product decisions should eventually be backed by real marketplace data.
